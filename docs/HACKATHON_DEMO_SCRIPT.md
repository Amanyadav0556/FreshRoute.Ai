# FreshRoute AI — Hackathon 3-Minute Winning Demo Script

## Hook (0:00 - 0:30): The Problem
> *"Every day in India, over ₹40,000 crores worth of perishable fruits and vegetables rot before ever reaching a consumer. In Maharashtra's Nashik-Mumbai agri corridor, farm-to-fork transit is a race against the clock. Current logistics relies on crude rules like First-Expired First-Out or chasing high spot prices, which often results in sending near-ripe produce down a 6-hour highway only to have it arrive spoiled. What if dispatch was scientifically optimized in milliseconds?"*

---

## The Solution (0:30 - 1:15): Live Dashboard & Optimization
> *"Meet **FreshRoute AI** — an intelligent perishable produce distribution and discount optimization engine.*
> 
> *[Switch to Overview Dashboard]*
> *Here on our live dashboard, distributors see real-time shelf life degradation across active produce batches. Using the Arrhenius equation and Q10 respiration curves, we model the exact biological decay caused by ambient heat and road vibration on ghat highways.*
> 
> *[Click on Optimization Planner]*
> *Watch this: when we run the FreshRoute Optimizer, SciPy's HiGHS LP solver evaluates every combination of produce batch, vehicle capacity, and buyer demand. Within 7 milliseconds, it computes an optimal dispatch plan that maximizes net economic value while guaranteeing every single truck arrives before the produce expires.*
> 
> *Every allocation comes with an automated plain-English explanation: why this batch of spinach was routed to Dadar market on a reefer truck, while tomatoes went to Vashi APMC."*

## Innovation: Dynamic Discount Engine (1:15 - 2:00)
> *"[Click on Discount Engine]*
> *What about produce with less than 18 hours of shelf life? > 
> *It shows distributors the exact trade-off between price velocity and waste recovery."*
